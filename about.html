I'm a Information System and Management student in Petropavl, Kazakhstan — building real products with Flutter, Python, and FastAPI while competing in hackathons and chasing international opportunities.

The story

From curiosity
to craft.

My journey started in 2025 when I enrolled in Information System and Management and wrote my first line of code. What began as a requirement became an obsession.

I built Chatra — a complete educational platform — from scratch, handling everything from the Flutter mobile app to the FastAPI backend and Nuxt.js web dashboard.

Today I'm competing in hackathons across Kazakhstan, studying Harvard's CS50P, and building toward international opportunities in tech.
