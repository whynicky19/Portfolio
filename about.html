The first line of code I ever wrote was in 2025 — Python for a university assignment. What started as coursework turned into a habit of building things at night and shipping them on weekends.

The first real product was Chatra Academy — a complete stack across Flutter, FastAPI, and Nuxt, deployed in production at my university. Two developers, three platforms, real students using it. That project taught me that design and engineering are the same conversation.

Today the focus has narrowed. I'm going deeper on Swift and SwiftUI, studying Apple's Human Interface Guidelines the way I used to study documentation, and writing native iOS interfaces that feel like the apps people keep on their home screen.

Outside the code: hackathons across Kazakhstan, a Harvard CS50P certificate earned by hand, and a first international conference presentation. The trajectory points outward.
