Featured case study · 01

A complete educational platform, shipped to real classrooms.

Problem

Educators ran classes through Telegram chats and spreadsheets. No unified tool. Students had no visibility into their own progress.

Design

Mobile-first UI for one-handed classroom use. Role-based interfaces for student, teacher, and admin.

Engineering

Three independent layers: Flutter mobile, FastAPI REST backend with JWT auth, Nuxt web dashboard with KZ/RU i18n. Docker-deployed.

Result

Live in real classrooms. Every layer — design, code, infrastructure — handled by two developers.
