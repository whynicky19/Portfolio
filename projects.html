Featured case study · 01

Chatra Academy.

2025 — 2026 · Production · iOS · Web · Backend

A complete educational platform, shipped to real classrooms.

Problem

At my university, educators ran classes through Telegram chats and spreadsheets. No unified tool. Students had no visibility into their own progress.

Design

Mobile-first UI designed for one-handed classroom use. Riverpod state, GoRouter navigation, role-based interfaces (student, teacher, admin).

Engineering

Three independent layers: Flutter mobile, FastAPI REST backend with JWT auth, Nuxt web dashboard with KZ/RU i18n. Docker-deployed.

Result

Live in real classrooms. Students join classes, submit assignments, track grades. Every layer — design, code, infra — handled by two developers.
